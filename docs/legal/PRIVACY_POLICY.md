This Privacy Policy explains what information the Steal Therapy mobile app
("STEEL", "the app", "we") collects, how it is used, and your choices. By using
the app you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

We only collect what the app needs to function. We do **not** sell your data and
we do **not** use advertising or third‑party analytics/tracking SDKs.

**a) Account information**
- Email address and a password (the password is transmitted to our backend and
  stored only as a secure hash; we never store it in plain text).

**b) Profile information you provide**
- Age, gender, height, body weight, fitness/experience level, training goal,
  training environment, available equipment, and optional injury/limitation
  notes.

**c) Training data you create**
- Workout plans and programs, training sessions, logged sets (weight, reps,
  RPE), mood/energy check‑ins, and session notes.

**d) Technical data**
- Standard connection metadata (e.g. IP address, request time) is processed by
  our backend server and content providers to deliver the service. We do not
  build advertising profiles from it.

We do **not** collect location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera, or device
identifiers for tracking. The app requests only the **INTERNET** permission.

## 2. How we use your information

- To create and authenticate your account.
- To generate, store, and display your plans, workouts, progress, and stats.
- To provide exercise content (names, instructions, demonstration GIFs) and
  translations in your selected language.
- To maintain security and prevent abuse of the service.

We process this data to perform the service you request (contract) and on the
basis of your consent, which you give when creating an account.

## 3. Where your data is stored and who can access it

- Your account, profile, and training data are stored on our **self‑hosted
  PocketBase backend**, controlled by the developer.
- Access is restricted to your authenticated account and the developer for
  operation and support.

## 4. Third‑party services

The app contacts the following third parties to function. Only the data
necessary for each request is shared; your account/profile data is **not** sent
to these providers.

- **Exercise content (ExerciseDB)** — exercise metadata and demonstration GIFs
  are fetched from a hosted ExerciseDB instance and its media CDN
  (`static.exercisedb.dev`). These requests contain exercise queries and your
  device's IP address, not your personal profile.
- **Google Fonts** — typefaces may be fetched from Google's font CDN
  (`fonts.gstatic.com`). Such requests include your device IP. See Google's
  privacy policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

If you later add other services (analytics, crash reporting, ads), update this
policy and your Play Console Data Safety form accordingly.

## 5. Data retention

We keep your account and training data until you delete your account or request
deletion. After deletion, your records are removed from the active database;
backups (if any) are rotated out on a routine schedule.

## 6. Your rights and choices

- **Access / correction:** view and edit your profile and training data in‑app.
- **Account & data deletion:** delete your account directly in the app
  (Gear / Settings → "Delete account"), which permanently removes your account
  and associated data. You may also request deletion by emailing [YOUR_EMAIL].
  See [ACCOUNT_DELETION_URL] for the deletion process.
- **Language:** the app interface and exercise content support English, Russian,
  and Uzbek; you can change this at any time in Settings.

Depending on your region (e.g. GDPR/EEA), you may also have rights to data
portability, restriction, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.
Contact [YOUR_EMAIL] to exercise these rights.

## 7. Children's privacy

The app is intended for general fitness audiences and is **not directed to
children under 13** (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not
knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has provided data,
contact [YOUR_EMAIL] and we will delete it.

## 8. Security

Passwords are stored as hashes and account access requires authentication. No
method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable
measures to protect your data. (Note: the app currently communicates with the
backend over HTTP scoped to a single host; migrating the backend to HTTPS is
planned and recommended.)
